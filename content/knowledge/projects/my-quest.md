## Context

Meta Quest games can be bought at a discount through referral links, but those deals are scattered and hard to find. I built a single place to browse them.

## What I built

A Next.js site with a catalogue of games:

- **Instant search and filtering**, on the client.
- **Trailers** that play when a card is hovered.
- **Five languages**, with localised routing.
- **A chat assistant** for questions, and **a newsletter** to hear about new deals.
- **Light and dark themes**, and a layout designed for mobile.
