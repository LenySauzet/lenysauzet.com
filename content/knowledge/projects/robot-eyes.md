## Context

OTO BOT is a visual experiment around robots. The eyes are what make a robot feel alive, so I explored how far a simple animation in the browser could carry that impression.

## What I built

Eyes that drift on their own and glance around at random, with a slow breathing glow, colours that change on command, and a sound for each movement, all in plain JavaScript, HTML and CSS.
