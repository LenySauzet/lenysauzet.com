## Context

My very first project at Epitech, and my first with Pygame: a classic game, used to build a complete little application in Python, from the game loop to the interface.

## What I built

A Pygame hangman that draws its secret words from NLTK's English corpus, so every game is different, lets the player choose a language and translates the game's messages into it, and adds animated characters, sound effects and a colourful interface.
