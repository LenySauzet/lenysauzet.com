question: Are you an AI engineer or a full-stack engineer?
topics: [profile, skills, ai, full-stack]

Both, on purpose. At Tolk.ai I started on the AI services and grew to the whole stack, and outside work I keep widening the range: back end, front end, web experiences and 3D, games, AI, and the tooling and workflows that make a team faster. Design interests me as much as the technique, and I look for inspiration on both sides.

What ties it together is the product. I like understanding how things work, proposing solutions, and making an experience fluid and fast, and the intelligence of a product, the part where AI does something useful, is where my two halves meet. I aim to be multidisciplinary: someone a team can put on any layer of a feature.
