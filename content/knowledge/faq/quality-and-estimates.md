---
id: quality-and-estimates
question: How do you approach deadlines and code quality?
topics: [workflow, quality, estimates, testing]
---

I take the time to build things that are clean, maintainable, fast and stable over the long run, rather than producing a lot at uneven quality.

Tests are part of how I work, all the more with AI-assisted development. They keep the code safe over time, with no breaking change slipping through, and they let an agent check and correct its own work before it comes back to me, instead of a person catching each mistake in another round of back and forth.

Nearly three years of shipping in short cycles taught me to estimate. I know much better what a feature really costs, I set my deadlines from that, and I say early when a scope does not fit instead of absorbing it.
