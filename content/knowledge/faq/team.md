---
id: team
question: What matters to you in a team and a workplace?
topics: [culture, team, career]
---

Being able to grow. I am curious and always learning, so I look for a place where I can take on more over time and learn from the people around me.

The people matter as much as the work. I like teams with an ambitious vision turned towards what comes next, made of people who are kind, inspiring, and easy to talk with openly, about the work and beyond it. Exchanging is how I find better solutions: I value code review, design discussions, and showing what I build. I enjoy presenting the features I work on and hearing what people think of them.

I work well on my own and like having room to explore, inside a team that is well organised and actually works together. The balance that suits me best is clear ownership with regular exchange.
