question: How do you keep learning?
topics: [learning, blog, interests]
---

Mostly by building. When a topic interests me, I make a project of it, and when I understand it well enough I write it up on my blog as a deep dive, with interactive figures the reader can play with. Explaining something is the best test I know of whether I really understand it.

I also take apart what others build: file formats, rendering tricks, state-of-the-art codebases, to see how they really work. I follow the designers, creative developers and 3D artists doing the best work on the web, and I rebuild course projects to widen my stack. My degree at Epitech, with an AI specialisation, gave me the foundations; curiosity does the rest.
