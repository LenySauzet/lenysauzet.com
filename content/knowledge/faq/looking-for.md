question: What kind of role and company are you looking for?
topics: [career, availability, company]
---

A permanent role as an AI engineer or a full-stack engineer, starting immediately, in Montpellier or fully remote. Those are the roles I know best rather than a fence: what I look for first is a product and a stack worth getting good at.

I am open to every kind of company. Nearly three years in a startup taught me to enjoy short iterations, a small team and a product that moves fast, and I would gladly do it again. I am just as curious about what I have not lived yet: a larger team, a more structured organisation, features scoped in more depth. A software publisher, a scale-up or a large group interests me as much as a startup.

What draws me in is the product and the technology first, then the domain, which matters when it gives me something to be proud of. I like useful products, ideally ones I would use myself, with customers who trust them and real stakes. Ambitious projects motivate me: I want to feel I am taking part in something that moves things forward.
