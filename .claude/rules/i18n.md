# Languages

English at `/`, French at `/fr`, on next-intl over Next 16's own pieces: an
`app/[locale]` root, `next/root-params`, `proxy.ts`. The blog stays in English.

## Routing

- **`i18n/routing.ts` decides it all**: `localePrefix: 'as-needed'` (`/en` redirects to
  `/`), no detection from `Accept-Language` or a cookie, no `Link` headers (the
  metadata declares the alternates). A visitor is never sent elsewhere than they asked.
- **Every route is under `app/[locale]`**, static per language through
  `generateStaticParams`, with `dynamicParams = false` so a segment that is no
  language, `/foo.txt` included, reaches `global-not-found` instead of a bare 404.
  `[...rest]` keeps its params dynamic, so `/fr/x` gets the French 404.
- **The proxy skips files and `opengraph-image`**: the share image is one, at the
  root, declared by the layout's metadata on both languages. Under `[locale]`, English
  pointed at `/en/opengraph-image`, which the prefix rule redirects.
- **`next/root-params` gives the locale** to `i18n/request.ts`; its types are
  generated, so `type-check` runs `next typegen` first. Route handlers cannot read it:
  `/resume` takes its `params`.

## Messages

- **`messages/en.json` is the type** (`i18n/app-config.d.ts`): a key used and missing
  fails the type-check. French is checked by `i18n/messages.test.ts`, same keys, same
  `{arguments}` and `<tags>`.
- **French is an edition, not a translation**: written in French, tool names left in
  English, a narrow no-break space before `?`, a no-break space before `:`, typographic
  apostrophes. Project titles are names and stay as they are.
- **Server components use `useTranslations`** or, when async, `getTranslations`; client
  ones get every message from `NextIntlClientProvider`. Code outside React (the
  commands, the services) receives its sentences, it never imports a translator.
- **Dates and times follow the page** (`useFormatter`, `Intl` with the locale): 24-hour
  time and `nov. 2026` in French.

## Between the two

- **Each page names the other** in the footer and the palette, in the other language,
  with `lang` (and `hrefLang` on the link) so it is read in its own voice.
- **`OtherVersion` offers it once**, through an island announcement four seconds in,
  after the hint, to a visitor whose first browser language is the other one; clicking
  the announcement switches. Remembered in `localStorage`, never shown again.
- **Each language has its resume** on the CDN (`siteConfig.resumes`) and its presence
  room (`.claude/rules/presence.md`).
